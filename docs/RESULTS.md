# Results and evidence boundaries

The Summer closeout (August 17, 2026) reports 3.797%, 3.633%, and 3.676% lower masked-gene reconstruction error for revealed-organ, hard-routed, and soft-routed specialists, respectively. All three fixed seeds improved on 821 samples across 63 connected studies and eight organs. Routing retained approximately 96–97% of the revealed-organ benefit.

The accessed ARCHS4 evaluation had a post-access QC amendment; it is not untouched confirmation. Learned representations did not beat raw expression or fold-fit PCA in the tested OSDR downstream benchmark. This limits the claim to reconstruction improvement under the stated evaluation.

The Fall status recorded September 15, 2026 reports within-flight associations without successful unseen-flight transfer. Irradiation-associated probe specificity was inadequate across stresses and tissues. Human-muscle controls do not support calling the flight classifier radiation-driven. No causal radiation fraction is established.

These are summaries of the supplied local reports. This source-only adaptation withholds original research artifacts and does not independently reproduce their metrics. Full replication requires approved inputs, original frozen manifests, checkpoint provenance, and the corresponding runtime.
