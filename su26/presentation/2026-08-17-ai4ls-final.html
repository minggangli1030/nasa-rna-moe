Future directions

Align the training task—then test it where there is room to improve

01 · Confirm

Repeat the strongest result

Use one completely untouched dataset to confirm the reconstruction benefit.

02 · Align

Train closer to the final question

Emphasize subtle biological differences within the same tissue—not only typical gene and tissue patterns.

03 · Test

Use a stronger biological test

Build a larger labeled dataset across several organs, with room to detect improvement.

Advance only ifThe model beats raw measurements and standard methods.Organ specialists beat an equally capable shared model.

The next step is to align what the model learns with downstream tasks that can deliver practical biological insight.
