Meaning

Under a fixed budget, recipient-specific data is more useful than any tested donor organ.

Not “all sharing is bad.” The prospectively frozen additive test asks whether B helps when A exposure is preserved.

08
Conclusion

Route by organ—and do not dilute scarce recipient data indiscriminately

What Stage 1 gives us

Replicated organ-specialist gains across routing forms, seeds, setups, and both train/test directions, with neutral capacity and random controls.

What Stage 2 adds

Every same-compute donor substitution causes negative transfer, supporting recipient-specific exposure and isolated specialists under limited budgets.

Next discriminating test: the already-frozen additive edges preserve all recipient exposure, separating “donor B adds information” from “donor B replaced more valuable A data.”

The result is seed- and donor-robust in GTEx development data, not yet universal across independent studies. Mechanism/router correspondence and a new untouched multisource confirmation remain ahead.
